By Alex Lawrence Traditional unit sorters have been a reliable part of distribution and fulfillment operations for decades. Tilt-tray, bombay, and cross-belt systems can deliver high throughput and consistent performance when product profiles, destinations, and volumes are relatively predictable. In the right environment, they remain an effective choice.
